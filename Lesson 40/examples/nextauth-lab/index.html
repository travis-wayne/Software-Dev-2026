🔑 Simulate Authentication Provider

NextAuth.js uses optional catch-all routing (/api/auth/[...nextauth].js) to automate multiple authentication workflows. Select a provider below to trace its serverless execution lifecycle!

Why use [...nextauth].js?

By placing three dots inside brackets in pages/api/auth/[...nextauth].js, Next.js dynamically catches any URL beginning with /api/auth/ and passes it to NextAuth without needing dozens of individual route files!

By default, NextAuth only sends public basic information (name, email, image) to the browser for security. To include your database id or administrative role, you must explicitly relay them through the jwt() and session() callbacks!
